Captionshaper 1.1.0: caption styles, rebuilt.

- Nine styles to start from: Clean, Subtitle box, Bold, Highlight, One word, Reveal, Neon, Comic and Minimal.
- Eleven caption fonts, including Montserrat, Poppins, Anton, Bebas Neue and Bangers.
- The spoken word can change colour or sit on a rounded box.
- Word animations: pop, fade in, slide up. Words not spoken yet can show, dim or stay hidden.
- Outline, shadow and glow, each with its own colour.
- The preview now matches the exported video exactly: every word is measured and placed by Captionshaper, with the same fonts, sizes and animation timing.
- Captions that would run off the screen shrink to fit, and vertical videos keep clear of the TikTok, Reels and Shorts buttons.
- Projects from 1.0 open with their style converted.

**Downloads**
- Mac (Apple Silicon): `Captionshaper-1.1.0-mac-arm64.dmg`. First time: right-click the app and choose Open.
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit): `Captionshaper-Setup-1.1.0-win-x64.exe`. If SmartScreen appears: More info, then Run anyway.
