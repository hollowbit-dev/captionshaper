Captionshaper 1.0.0: free, offline auto-subtitles for Mac and Windows.

- Drop in a video or audio file. Speech is recognised on your computer (Whisper Large v3 Turbo through whisper.cpp; GPU on Apple Silicon).
- Words appear live while it works.
- No captions over music or silence (voice activity detection), word-level timing, and line breaks that read well.
- Edit in place, find and replace, split, merge, undo. Uncertain words are underlined.
- Styles: Clean, Box, Pop and Word by word, with a phone view for vertical videos.
- Export SRT, VTT, ASS, TXT, or an MP4 with captions burned in.
- 99 languages, detected automatically, plus translation to English.

**Downloads**
- Mac (Apple Silicon): `Captionshaper-1.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg`. First time: right-click the app and choose Open.
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit): `Captionshaper-Setup-1.0.0-win-x64.exe`. If SmartScreen appears: More info, then Run anyway.

The speech model (about 570 MB) downloads once on first use. Nothing else connects to the internet.
