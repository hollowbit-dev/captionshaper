Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no watermark, no limits. Captionshaper is open source, and you can support it on Ko-fi if it saves you time.

Does it upload my video?

No. Everything happens on your computer. The app downloads the speech model once (about 570 MB), and that's its only connection to the internet.

Which languages does it understand?

About 99, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese and Japanese. It detects the language for you. For less common languages, the Most accurate model makes fewer mistakes.

How accurate is it?

On clear speech it gets almost every word right. We test it on clips with known scripts in English, Spanish and German: 0.7% word error rate, with captions within 0.15 seconds of the voice. Noisy audio and strong accents make more mistakes, which is why fixing words is quick.

Can it translate?

It can turn speech in any supported language into English captions. Tick “Translate to English” before you start.

Is it fast on Windows?

It runs on the processor, about one to two times real time on a recent PC. On Apple Silicon Macs it uses the GPU and is around five times faster than real time.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not yet. The Mac version needs Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).
