Changelog

Every release of Captionshaper, newest first.

1.1.011 November 2026

Caption styles, rebuilt

  • Nine styles: Clean, Subtitle box, Bold, Highlight, One word, Reveal, Neon, Comic and Minimal.
  • Eleven caption fonts, including Montserrat, Poppins, Anton, Bebas Neue and Bangers.
  • The spoken word can change colour or sit on a rounded box.
  • Word animations: pop, fade in and slide up. Words not spoken yet can show, dim or stay hidden.
  • Outline, shadow and glow, each with its own colour.
  • The preview now matches the exported video exactly.
1.0.111 November 2026

New look

  • Black and electric lime design with a new icon.
  • The spoken word in the caption list is easier to read.
1.0.011 November 2026

First release

  • Offline speech recognition with word-level timing in about 99 languages.
  • Voice detection keeps captions off music and silence.
  • Words appear live while transcribing.
  • Caption editor with waveform timeline, find and replace, split, merge and undo.
  • Phone view for vertical videos.
  • Export SRT, VTT, ASS, TXT, or an MP4 with captions burned in.
