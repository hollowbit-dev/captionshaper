It listens properly

Speech recognition runs on your Mac or PC with OpenAI's Whisper model, so it understands accents, fast talkers and around 99 languages.

  • Finds the speech firstVoice detection skips intros, music and silence, so no words get invented over a beat drop.
  • Timed to the wordEach word starts when the voice does. Captions appear in sync, and word highlights land on the beat.
  • Detects the languageOr pick it yourself. It can also turn any supported language into English captions.
  • Three modelsRecommended for most videos, Most accurate for harder languages and audio, Fast for quick drafts.

Captions that read well

A transcript isn't captions. Captionshaper decides where every caption starts and ends, and where each line breaks.

  • Natural line breaksBreaks fall at sentence and clause ends, never after “the”, “a” or “to”.
  • Never across a pauseWhen the speaker stops, the caption stops. Lines fit the width of your video.
  • Easy to fixWords it isn't sure about are underlined. Edit in place, find and replace, split, merge and undo.
  • Saved as you goClose the app and open the same video later: your edits are still there.

Styles made for social video

Nine looks to start from and eleven caption fonts. The preview is exactly what gets exported.

  • Highlight the spoken wordChange its colour or put it on a box, the way the big creators do.
  • Word animationsPop, fade in or slide up. Hide or dim the words that haven't been said yet.
  • Outline, shadow and glowEach with its own colour, plus a background box behind lines.
  • Phone viewSee where TikTok, Reels and Shorts put their buttons, so captions never hide under them.

Every way out

Take your captions wherever they need to go.

  • Video with captionsAn MP4 with the captions burned in, ready to post.
  • Subtitle filesSRT for YouTube and video editors, VTT for the web, ASS with your full style.
  • TranscriptPlain text, split into paragraphs at long pauses.
  • Private by designNothing is uploaded. After the one-time model download, it works offline.
