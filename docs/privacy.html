Privacy

Captionshaper runs on your computer. Your videos, audio, captions and edits stay there.

The app makes one kind of network request: downloading a speech model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you click Download. That request doesn't include anything about your files.

There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself has no cookies or analytics.
